# Evolution

Uteki's archive is a sequence of research-system snapshots, not conventional
software releases.

| Snapshot | Cutoff | Central question | What carries forward |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Early agent/product exploration | 2026-01-21 | How can an agent help investors? | Product intuition and failed paths |
| Research workflow exploration | 2026-05-31 | How should an agent conduct research? | Workflow and handoff lessons |
| Research infrastructure prototype | 2026-08-25 | What data and workflow does research require? | Data Agent, evidence-backed facts, company workflow, thesis tracking |
| Uteki Beta | 2026-09-06 onward | How do we know the research system works? | Evaluation-first development |

The archived implementations remain evidence of how the understanding changed.
They should be mined selectively, never copied wholesale into this repository.

The next retrospective should explain the snapshots in reverse order (what
each produced), then forward order (what changed in our understanding).
